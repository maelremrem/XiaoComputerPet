Desk Buddy

Life & interactions

Speech is limited to two words. Ambient idle animations stay silent and never ask for attention; contextual bubbles only react to meaningful events.

Hold a side = scratch · sweep left/right = pet · both sides = hug. Ambient animations include silent yawns, sneezes, hiccups, dreams and tiny dances.
